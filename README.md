# AI-ChatNote
Comment on individual paragraphs in AI chat responses before sending your next message.

AI-ChatNote is a free browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that lets you add inline comments to individual paragraphs or bullet points, then process them all at once for faster, better results.

Hover over a paragraph or bullet point to highlight it, click to add a comment, and move on. When you're done, hit Return and all your comments are applied in a single pass.

Works with all major AI platforms, among them OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI and X.

Caution: Switching models during a chat session disables ChatNote. You can reactivate it by reloading the page (F5).

All data remains local to your browser. The extension only runs on the supported chat URLs listed in the manifest and does not transmit any information to external servers.
